About the challenge:
From intelligent automation and emerging technologies to environmental sustainability and social impact, Neural Storm challenges participants to apply AI and ML to meaningful real-world problems.

Participants can choose from three official challenge tracks, featuring 15 curated problem statements, or define their own innovative problem within their chosen track
Explore the Three Challenge Tracks
1. Future Tech — Intelligent Systems & Emerging Technology

Push the boundaries of intelligent automation, computer vision, natural language processing, and next-generation technologies.

2. Green Planet — Sustainability & Environmental Intelligence

Harness AI to tackle environmental challenges, conserve natural resources, and build a more sustainable future.

3. Human Impact — Healthcare, Education & Social Good

Create accessible, responsible, and impactful AI solutions that improve people's lives across healthcare, education, financial inclusion, and citizen services.

The Challenge
You have 48 hours to identify a problem, develop an AI-powered solution, build a functional prototype, and demonstrate its potential impact.

Whether you're training a machine learning model, building an intelligent application, or integrating AI into an innovative system, Neural Storm is your opportunity to turn ideas into reality.

Think boldly. Build intelligently. Make an impact.

What to Build
At Neural Storm 2026, participants must develop an AI/ML-powered project under one of the three official challenge tracks.

What to Submit
Ready to showcase your innovation? Submit your completed Neural Storm 2026 project before the official deadline.

Required Submission
1. Public GitHub Repository

Provide a public GitHub repository containing your complete project source code.

Your repository must include a comprehensive README.md with:

Project title and problem statement number

Team name and member names

Problem description and proposed solution

Technology stack and libraries used

Setup and installation instructions

Instructions for running the project locally

Screenshots or a demo GIF of the working project

Model accuracy or performance metrics, where applicable

Disclosure of any AI development tools used, including ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or similar tools

Strongly Recommended
2. Live Deployment Link

Provide a working link to your deployed application, dashboard, or interactive prototype.

A live demonstration helps judges evaluate your solution and is strongly recommended.

You may use platforms such as Streamlit Cloud, Hugging Face Spaces, or Render.

Submission Guidelines
Only one submission is permitted per team.

Submissions must be completed before November 4, 2026, at 8:00 PM IST.

Teams may update their projects and resubmit before the deadline.

Late submissions will not be accepted.

Projects must follow the official Neural Storm 2026 rules.

Before submitting: Verify that your GitHub repository is public, your README is complete, and your project can be demonstrated successfully.

Judging Criteria
Technical Implementation
Quality of code, use of AI/ML techniques, model accuracy, and overall technical depth.
Impact & Usefulness
Does the solution have real-world applicability? How significant is the problem being solved?
README & Documentation
Clarity of the README: setup instructions, problem statement, approach, results, and screenshots.
Demo & Presentation
Working demo (live or video), deployment link if available, and overall presentation quality.
